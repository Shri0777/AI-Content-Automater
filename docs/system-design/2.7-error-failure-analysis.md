## 2.7.1 Purpose

### Why Systematic Failure Analysis?

Failure is not an exceptional technical detail—it is a **first-class business concern** in the AI Content Automater system.

The system interacts with multiple external services (AI providers, research sources, social platforms), operates asynchronously, manages stateful workflows across mandatory quality gates, and must accurately represent outcomes to users. Failures at any point can:

- Block content from progressing through mandatory workflows
- Cause financial waste through unbounded retries
- Produce duplicate publications
- Falsely report success when operations failed
- Lose track of which platforms succeeded vs. failed
- Bypass mandatory quality or authorization gates
- Corrupt business state through inconsistent updates

**This analysis addresses:**

**WHAT can fail** — Identify all meaningful failure points in the system

**HOW failures affect business state** — Determine which domain objects, workflows, and data are impacted

**WHAT must be preserved** — Ensure accurate failure representation without silent data corruption

**HOW the system must respond** — Define required system behavior (block, retry, notify, record, require intervention)

**WHAT remains uncertain** — Distinguish known failures from unknown external states

**WHERE human intervention is required** — Identify failures requiring user or administrator action

## 2.7.2 Failure Analysis Principles

### Core Principles

**1. Failures Must Be Explicit**

Failures shall not be hidden, ignored, or implicitly converted to success.

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 14:** "If required research fails, the system shall record the failure; preserve useful diagnostics; avoid marking research successful."

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 16:** "Timeouts, invalid responses, malformed responses, and provider errors shall never be treated as successful generation."

**REQUIREMENTS.MD BR-013:** "Failed external operations must not be represented as successful."

---

**2. Failures Must Not Silently Corrupt Business State**

A failed operation shall never leave business objects in inconsistent or falsely successful states.

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 11:** "A failed stage shall never silently advance content."

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 50:** "A failed task shall not leave its business entity falsely marked complete."

---

**3. Unknown External State Must Remain Unknown**

When the system cannot determine whether an external operation succeeded or failed, it must represent the state as UNKNOWN, not guess.

**REQUIREMENTS.MD BR-018:** "Unknown external state must be represented explicitly rather than guessed."

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 47:** "Unknown external state shall never be silently converted to success or failure without evidence."

**Critical Distinction:**
- **Known Failure** — Operation attempted, platform/service confirmed failure
- **Unknown State** — Operation attempted, outcome cannot be determined (timeout, lost connection, ambiguous response)
- **Known Success** — Operation attempted, platform/service confirmed success

Unknown ≠ Failure
Unknown ≠ Success

---

**4. Retry Must Be Bounded Where Required**

The system shall not retry operations indefinitely.

**REQUIREMENTS.MD BR-012:** "Publishing retries must be bounded and duplicate-safe."

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 37:** "The system shall not retry indefinitely."

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 21:** "Automated correction/review must be finite."

Unbounded retry risks:
- Infinite cost accumulation
- Resource exhaustion
- Duplicate operations
- Indefinite blocking of workflows

---

**5. Retry Must Be Safe / Idempotent**

Retries shall not cause duplicate business effects.

**REQUIREMENTS.MD BR-012:** "Publishing retries must be bounded and duplicate-safe."

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 35:** "Each publishing job shall have a stable identity for duplicate detection."

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 29:** "Concurrent workers shall not claim the same publish operation in a way that can cause duplicate publication."

---

**6. Partial Success Must Remain Visible**

Multi-platform publishing creates independent per-platform results. Platform A success must not be hidden by Platform B failure.

**REQUIREMENTS.MD BR-010:** "Publishing outcomes are tracked independently per platform."

**REQUIREMENTS.MD BR-011:** "A failed platform publication must not overwrite another platform's successful result."

---

**7. Mandatory Quality and Authorization Controls Cannot Be Bypassed by Failure Handling**

Failure recovery must not circumvent mandatory gates.

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 11:** "Invalid state transitions shall be rejected."

**REQUIREMENTS.MD BR-002:** "Mandatory AI reviews must occur before final validation" (failures cannot skip reviews).

**REQUIREMENTS.MD BR-008:** "Scheduled content must be revalidated at execution time" (even if previously valid).

---

**8. Failures Must Remain Traceable**

Failed operations require audit and history records.

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 44:** "The system shall maintain an audit trail for security-sensitive and workflow-significant events."

**REQUIREMENTS.MD Section 50:** "Background tasks shall preserve debugging context."

---

**9. Unresolved Recovery Policies Must Not Be Invented**

If REQUIREMENTS.MD does not specify recovery behavior, it must be documented as unresolved, not assumed.

## 2.7.3 Failure Taxonomy

Based on REQUIREMENTS.MD V2.0, the following failure categories exist:

### Authentication Failures

**Definition:** User authentication fails

**Examples:**
- Invalid credentials
- Expired session
- Missing authentication

**Business Impact:** User cannot access protected functionality

**System Response (Section 7):**
- Authentication failures shall not disclose sensitive information
- Request denied

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-001 (User)

**Requirements:** FR-001, Section 7

---

### Authorization Failures

**Definition:** Authenticated user lacks permission for requested operation

**Examples:**
- User attempts to access another user's content (BR-001 violation)
- User attempts administrative operation without admin privileges
- User attempts to approve without approval authority (authorization rules not fully specified)

**Business Impact:** Unauthorized operation blocked

**System Response (Section 8):**
- Unauthorized requests shall fail safely without exposing protected data
- Authorization enforced server-side
- Access denied

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-001 (User), DC-002 (Administrator), all user-owned resources

**Requirements:** FR-002, BR-001, Section 8

---

### User Input Validation Failures

**Definition:** User-provided input fails validation

**Examples:**
- Missing required fields
- Invalid data types
- Invalid content length
- Invalid format

**Business Impact:** Operation rejected, user must correct input

**System Response (Section 55):**
- All externally supplied data shall be validated before use
- Validation covers: required fields, data types, allowed values, ownership, lifecycle state, platform compatibility

**Affected Domain Concepts:** All user-created entities (Content, Campaign, Schedule, Media, etc.)

**Requirements:** FR-022, Section 55

---

### Research Failures

**Definition:** Research collection from external sources fails

**Examples:**
- Research source unavailable
- Research timeout
- Invalid research result
- Unable to retrieve research
- Research source error

**Business Impact:**
- Research marked as failed (not successful)
- Dependent generation blocked when research is mandatory (Section 14)
- Actionable failure state exposed

**System Response (Section 14):**
1. Record the failure
2. Preserve useful diagnostics
3. Avoid marking research successful
4. **Prevent dependent generation when research is mandatory**
5. Expose an actionable failure state
6. **Transient failures may use bounded retries**
7. **System shall never fabricate research results**

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-005 (Research), DC-006 (AI Generation - blocked)

**Requirements:** FR-005, BR-013, Section 14

**Retryable:** Yes (bounded, for transient failures)

**Unresolved Decisions:**
- Exact retry limits for research
- Definition of "transient" vs. "permanent" research failure
- Whether research is mandatory vs. optional (context-dependent)

---

### AI Generation Failures

**Definition:** AI content generation fails

**Examples:**
- Generation service unavailable
- Generation timeout
- Invalid AI response
- Malformed AI response
- AI provider error
- Empty/unusable generated content

**Business Impact:**
- Generation marked as failed (not successful)
- No new content version created
- Dependent AI Review #1 NOT initiated (Section 16)
- Content remains in previous state
- Actionable failure state exposed

**System Response (Section 16):**
1. Record the failure
2. Do NOT mark generation successful
3. **Do NOT begin dependent review**
4. Expose a useful failure state
5. **Use bounded retry only when safe**
6. **Timeouts, invalid responses, malformed responses, provider errors SHALL NEVER be treated as successful generation**

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-003 (Content), DC-006 (AI Generation), DC-007 (AI Review - NOT initiated)

**Requirements:** FR-006, BR-013, Section 16

**Retryable:** Yes (bounded, only when safe)

**Critical:**
Generation failure does NOT automatically create a "failed" content version.
Content remains in state before generation was attempted.

---

### AI Review Failures (Technical)

**Definition:** AI review operation itself fails (service unavailable, timeout, error)

**Examples:**
- Review service unavailable
- Review timeout
- Invalid review response
- Review service error

**Business Impact:**
- Review marked as failed (not completed)
- Content cannot advance to next stage
- Retry may be appropriate (bounded)

**System Response:**
- Record technical failure
- Do NOT mark review as completed
- Preserve content version for retry
- Expose actionable failure state

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-007 (AI Review), DC-004 (Content Version - review incomplete)

**Requirements:** FR-007 (Review #1), FR-009 (Review #2), BR-002, BR-013

**Retryable:** Likely yes (bounded), but not explicitly specified

**Critical Distinction:**
**Technical Review Failure** (service unavailable) ≠ **Successful Review That Rejects Content** (review completed, found issues)

---

### AI Review Rejection (Business Outcome)

**Definition:** AI review successfully completes but identifies unacceptable content requiring correction

**Examples:**
- Review #1 identifies correctable issues
- Review #2 identifies issues requiring additional correction
- Content fails quality criteria

**Business Impact:**
- Review succeeded as operation (not a technical failure)
- Review outcome: content requires correction
- Correction workflow triggered (if under revision limit)
- Bounded revision rules apply (BR-003)

**System Response (Section 17, 18, 20):**
- Review result recorded (issues identified)
- Correction initiated (if under revision limit per BR-003)
- Content proceeds to correction workflow
- Revision counter incremented

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-007 (AI Review), DC-008 (Correction), DC-009 (Revision Counter)

**Requirements:** FR-007, FR-009, BR-002

**Retryable:** Via correction workflow (bounded by BR-003)

**Critical:**
This is NOT a failure—it is a valid business outcome. Review succeeded; content requires improvement.

---

### Correction Failures

**Definition:** AI correction operation fails

**Examples:**
- Correction service unavailable
- Correction timeout
- Invalid correction result
- Correction produces unusable content

**Business Impact:**
- Correction marked as failed
- No new corrected version created
- Content remains in previous version state
- Revision counter may or may not be incremented (requirements unclear)
- Retry may be appropriate (bounded)

**System Response:**
- Record correction failure
- Preserve previous version
- Do NOT create invalid corrected version
- Expose actionable failure state

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-008 (Correction), DC-004 (Content Version - correction incomplete), DC-009 (Revision Counter)

**Requirements:** FR-008, BR-003, Section 19

**Retryable:** Possibly (bounded), but not explicitly specified

**Unresolved Decisions:**
- Does correction failure consume a revision attempt?
- Exact retry behavior for correction failures

---

### Revision Exhaustion

**Definition:** Automated correction/review cycles reach configured maximum limit

**Examples:**
- Revision counter reaches maximum (exact value unspecified, Section 74)
- Review #2 identifies issues but limit exhausted
- AI cannot produce acceptable content within bounds

**Business Impact:**
- **Automated revision STOPS** (Section 21)
- **Automatic publishing BLOCKED** (Section 21)
- Workflow enters **Intervention Required** state (Section 21)
- **Reason recorded** (Section 21)
- **Human intervention required**

**System Response (Section 21, BR-003):**
1. Stop automated revision
2. Block automatic publishing
3. Enter intervention or failed state
4. Record reason (revision limit exhausted)
5. Notify user (likely, though not explicitly stated)

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-009 (Revision Counter), DC-003 (Content - intervention required state)

**Requirements:** FR-010, BR-003, Section 21

**Retryable:** No (automated revision exhausted)

**Recovery:** Requires human intervention (manual edit, different approach, or abandonment)

**Critical:**
This is a **bounded automation safety mechanism**, not a technical failure. System correctly prevents unbounded AI revision loops.

---

### Final Validation Failures

**Definition:** Final validation before approval fails

**Examples:**
- Required fields missing
- Content fails platform compatibility checks
- Media validation fails
- Length/format rules violated
- Approval conditions not met
- Other business rules violated

**Business Impact:**
- Validation marked as failed
- Content NOT eligible for approval
- Publishing BLOCKED (BR-005)
- User must address validation failures

**System Response (Section 24, BR-005):**
- Record validation failure with specific findings
- Block advancement to approval
- Block publication (BR-004, BR-005)
- Expose validation findings to user

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-023 (Validation Result), DC-013 (Publishing Eligibility - not eligible), DC-010 (Approval - cannot be granted)

**Requirements:** FR-011, BR-004, BR-005, Section 24

**Retryable:** After user corrects issues

**Recovery:** User fixes validation failures, revalidates

---

### Approval Failures

**Definition:** Approval operation fails or approval state becomes invalid

**Examples:**
- Unauthorized user attempts approval
- Approval attempted for invalid content version
- Approval attempted for content that failed validation
- Material edit invalidates previous approval (BR-007)

**Business Impact:**
- Approval not granted OR approval invalidated
- Content not eligible for scheduling/publishing
- User must obtain valid approval

**System Response:**
- Unauthorized approval: blocked (authorization failure)
- Invalid version approval: blocked
- Material edit: **previous approval invalidated** (BR-007, Section 12)
- Invalidation reason recorded

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-010 (Approval), DC-004 (Content Version), DC-013 (Publishing Eligibility)

**Requirements:** FR-012, BR-006, BR-007, Section 25

**Retryable:** After addressing issue (revalidate, obtain proper authorization, re-approve new version)

**Critical:**
Material edit creating new version N+1 invalidates approval for version N (BR-007). This prevents publishing unapproved changes.

---

### Scheduling Failures

**Definition:** Content scheduling fails or scheduled content becomes invalid

**Examples:**
- Unauthorized scheduling attempt
- Content not eligible when scheduled (missing approval, failed validation)
- Invalid timezone (Section 28)
- Invalid execution time
- Scheduled content becomes ineligible before execution (BR-008)

**Business Impact:**
- Schedule not created OR execution refused
- Content not published at scheduled time
- User notified of scheduling failure

**System Response:**
- Invalid schedule: blocked, not created
- **Execution-time revalidation (BR-008):**
  - At scheduled time, re-check ALL eligibility conditions (Section 29)
  - If ineligible: **refuse publication**, keep schedule pending (Section 27)
  - Record outcome

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-011 (Schedule), DC-013 (Publishing Eligibility), DC-003 (Content)

**Requirements:** FR-013, BR-006, BR-008, Section 27, Section 28, Section 29

**Retryable:** Scheduler may retry later if eligibility is restored

**Critical:**
**Execution-time revalidation (BR-008)** is mandatory. Previously valid content may become invalid (approval revoked, content edited, platform authorization expired, media deleted).

---

### Campaign Configuration Failures

**Definition:** Campaign creation or configuration fails

**Examples:**
- Invalid campaign configuration
- Missing required campaign fields
- Unauthorized campaign creation

**Business Impact:**
- Campaign not created
- User must correct configuration

**System Response:**
- Validation failure reported
- Campaign not created
- User corrects and retries

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-012 (Campaign)

**Requirements:** FR-014, Section 30

**Retryable:** After user corrects configuration

---

### Campaign Execution Failures

**Definition:** Individual campaign execution iteration fails

**Examples:**
- Campaign disabled when execution scheduled (BR-015)
- Content generation fails during campaign execution
- AI workflow fails during campaign execution
- Approval fails during campaign execution
- Publishing fails during campaign execution

**Business Impact:**
- **Single execution fails**
- Campaign itself may continue (Section 31)
- Failed execution recorded
- Campaign continues unless user disables (Section 31)

**System Response (Section 31):**
- Record execution failure
- **Campaign failures recorded without silently disabling unrelated campaigns** (Section 31)
- Campaign continues executing future iterations (unless user disables)
- Each generated content item individually traceable (Section 30)

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-014 (Campaign Execution), DC-012 (Campaign - continues unless disabled)

**Requirements:** FR-014, BR-014, BR-015, Section 30, Section 31

**Retryable:** Next campaign execution attempts at next scheduled time

**Critical Distinction:**
**Individual Execution Failure** (one iteration fails) ≠ **Campaign Failure** (campaign itself terminates)

Requirements state campaign continues unless user disables it (BR-015, Section 31).

---

### Social Platform Authorization Failures

**Definition:** Platform connection or authorization fails

**Examples:**
- OAuth authorization fails
- Platform credentials expired
- Platform credentials revoked
- Platform API key invalid

**Business Impact:**
- Platform connection not established OR connection becomes invalid
- Publishing to affected platform BLOCKED
- User must reauthorize

**System Response (Section 33):**
- **Expired or revoked credentials produce clear integration state** (Section 33)
- Publishing eligibility check fails for affected platform
- **Failure of one platform authorization does NOT invalidate unrelated platform connections** (Section 33)
- User notified to reauthorize

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-015 (Social Account Connection), DC-013 (Publishing Eligibility - blocked for affected platform)

**Requirements:** FR-015, BR-009, Section 33

**Retryable:** After user reauthorizes platform

**Recovery:** User reconnects platform account

---

### Publishing Failures

**Definition:** Content publication to platform fails

**Examples:**
- Platform API unavailable
- Platform rejects content
- Authentication expired
- Network failure
- Rate limit exceeded
- Invalid content for platform
- Platform error

**Business Impact:**
- Publishing marked as **FAILED** (not successful)
- Platform-specific result recorded as Failed (BR-013)
- **Independent per-platform** (BR-010, BR-011)
- Retry may be appropriate (bounded, BR-012)

**System Response (Section 36, BR-013):**
1. **Record failure accurately** (BR-013)
2. Record failure category: transient vs. permanent (Section 36)
3. Preserve safe platform response information
4. **Failed attempt never recorded as successful** (BR-013, Section 36)
5. **Distinguish transient and permanent failures** (Section 36)
6. **Independent result per platform** (BR-010, BR-011)
7. May trigger bounded retry (BR-012)

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-017 (Publishing Attempt), DC-018 (Platform Publishing Result - Failed)

**Requirements:** FR-015, FR-017, BR-010, BR-011, BR-012, BR-013, Section 36, Section 37

**Retryable:** Yes (bounded, per BR-012)

**Failure Categories (Section 36):**
- Transient failures: Network issues, timeouts, rate limits (may retry)
- Permanent failures: Content rejected, invalid credentials (do not retry as ordinary failures - Section 37)

---

### Multi-Platform Partial Failures

**Definition:** Content targets multiple platforms; some succeed, some fail

**Example Scenario (Section 34):**
```
Content Publishing Attempt
  ├─ Platform A: Published ✓
  ├─ Platform B: Failed ✗
  └─ Platform C: Published ✓

Overall Status: Partially-Successful
```

**Business Impact:**
- **Platform A result: Published** (success recorded)
- **Platform B result: Failed** (failure recorded)
- **Platform C result: Published** (success recorded)
- **Overall status: Partially-Successful**
- User sees accurate per-platform outcomes
- Retry can target only Platform B (failed platform)

**System Response (BR-010, BR-011, Section 34):**
1. **Each platform result stored independently** (BR-010)
2. **Platform A success NOT overwritten by Platform B failure** (BR-011)
3. **Platform results immutable once recorded** (BR-011)
4. Overall status: Partially-Successful (Section 34)
5. Retry targets only failed platform (BR-012)

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-017 (Publishing Attempt), DC-018 (Platform Publishing Result - independent per platform), DC-021 (Overall Publishing Status - Partially-Successful)

**Requirements:** FR-016, BR-010, BR-011, Section 34

**Retryable:** Failed platforms only (bounded, BR-012)

**Critical:**
**BR-011 enforcement is CRITICAL.** Platform A's confirmed success must NEVER be lost or overwritten because Platform B failed later.

---

### Unknown External State

**Definition:** System cannot determine whether external operation succeeded or failed

**Examples:**
- Publishing request sent, timeout occurs before response
- Connection lost after request sent
- Platform response ambiguous
- Worker crashes during critical operation

**Business Impact:**
- External publishing state **UNKNOWN** (not success, not failure)
- System cannot definitively know if content was published
- Requires investigation or reconciliation
- User notified of uncertain state

**System Response (BR-018, Section 47):**
1. **Do NOT assume success**
2. **Do NOT assume failure**
3. **Record state as "Unknown"** (BR-018)
4. **Unknown state SHALL NOT be silently converted to success or failure** (Section 47)
5. Preserve enough information for investigation
6. May require manual investigation or query to platform (if query API available)

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-018 (Platform Publishing Result - Unknown)

**Requirements:** BR-018, Section 47

**Retryable:** Uncertain (may cause duplicate if retry attempted blindly)

**Recovery:** Requires investigation, may require:
- Query to platform API (if available) to determine actual state
- Manual user investigation
- Time-based assumption (e.g., assume failure after X time)

**Critical:**
**Unknown ≠ Success**
**Unknown ≠ Failure**

Unknown state is explicit, legitimate outcome when external state cannot be confirmed.

**Unresolved Decisions:**
- Exact resolution process for unknown publishing states (Section 74 does not specify)
- Whether system attempts automatic reconciliation
- How long unknown states persist before resolution

### Publishing Retry Exhaustion

**Definition:** Bounded publishing retries exhaust without success

**Examples:**
- Maximum retry count reached (exact limit unspecified, Section 74)
- All retry attempts failed
- Transient failure becomes permanent after retries

**Business Impact:**
- Publishing marked as **permanently failed**
- No further automatic retries
- User notified
- Manual intervention may be required

**System Response (Section 37, BR-012):**
1. Stop retrying (bounded per BR-012)
2. Mark publishing as permanently failed
3. Record final outcome
4. Notify user

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-019 (Publishing Retry - exhausted), DC-018 (Platform Publishing Result - Failed)

**Requirements:** FR-017, BR-012, Section 37

**Retryable:** No (retry limit exhausted)

**Recovery:** Manual retry, fix underlying issue, or abandon

**Unresolved Decisions:**
- Exact retry limits (Section 74)
- Exact retry delays and backoff values (Section 74)

---

### Duplicate Publishing Risk

**Definition:** Risk of publishing same content multiple times to same platform

**Examples:**
- Retry after uncertain outcome
- Concurrent workers claim same job
- Scheduler duplication defect
- Network retry after initial success

**Business Impact:**
- Same content published multiple times (undesirable)
- User confusion
- Platform policy violations
- Potential cost implications

**System Response (BR-012, Section 29, Section 35):**
1. **Each publishing job has stable identity** (Section 35)
2. **Concurrent workers shall NOT claim same job** (Section 29)
3. **Platform idempotency features used where available** (Section 35)
4. **Application-level locking and uniqueness constraints** (Section 35)
5. **Retries preserve duplicate-publication protection** (BR-012, Section 37)

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-017 (Publishing Attempt - stable job identity)

**Requirements:** BR-012, Section 29, Section 35

**Mitigation:** Stable job identity, idempotency, locking, uniqueness

---

### Media Validation Failures

**Definition:** Media fails validation before publication

**Examples:**
- Media missing
- Media invalid
- Media does not meet platform requirements
- Unsupported media type
- Media too large

**Business Impact:**
- **Missing or invalid required media blocks affected platform publication** (Section 39)
- **Media failure for one platform does NOT invalidate another platform** (Section 39)
- Content not eligible for affected platform

**System Response (Section 39):**
- Record media validation failure
- Block affected platform publication
- Other platforms unaffected (independent)
- User must fix media issue

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-019 (Media), DC-013 (Publishing Eligibility - blocked for affected platform)

**Requirements:** FR-019, BR-005, Section 39

**Retryable:** After user corrects media

**Recovery:** User uploads valid media or removes affected platform

---

### Notification Delivery Failures

**Definition:** Notification to user fails to deliver

**Examples:**
- Notification service unavailable
- Invalid notification address
- Notification delivery timeout

**Business Impact:**
- User not informed of event
- **Primary business operation outcome unchanged** (notification failure does NOT change publishing result, approval state, etc.)

**System Response:**
- Record notification delivery failure
- May retry notification delivery (bounded)
- **Do NOT change underlying business result**

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-022 (Notification)

**Requirements:** FR-018, Section 38

**Retryable:** Likely yes (bounded), but not explicitly specified

**Critical:**
Notification delivery failure must NOT:
- Change publishing result from Failed to Success
- Change approval state
- Affect primary operation outcome

Notification is **secondary communication**, not primary business operation.

---

### Analytics Computation Failures

**Definition:** Derived analytics metrics fail to compute

**Examples:**
- Analytics query fails
- External platform analytics unavailable
- Analytics aggregation error

**Business Impact:**
- Analytics unavailable or incomplete
- **Operational business truth UNAFFECTED**
- User sees incomplete analytics

**System Response (Section 40):**
- **Unavailable metrics shall never be fabricated** (Section 40)
- Analytics failure recorded
- Operational data (publishing results, content state) remains accurate
- May retry analytics computation

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-024 (Analytics Data - derived)

**Requirements:** FR-020, Section 40

**Retryable:** Yes (analytics derived from operational data)

**Critical:**
Analytics failure must NOT:
- Overwrite confirmed publishing results
- Change operational content state
- Fabricate metrics (Section 40)

**Analytics data is DERIVED**, not source-of-truth.

### Background Job Failures

**Definition:** Asynchronous background task fails

**Examples:**
- Worker crash
- Task timeout
- Unhandled exception
- Resource unavailability

**Business Impact:**
- Background operation incomplete
- **Business entity NOT falsely marked complete** (Section 50)
- Job failure recorded
- May retry (bounded)

**System Response (Section 50, BR-017):**
1. Report failure (Section 50)
2. **Do NOT leave business entity falsely marked complete** (Section 50)
3. Handle expected exceptions (Section 50)
4. Use bounded retries (Section 50)
5. Preserve debugging context (Section 50)
6. **Preserve ownership context** (Section 9)

**Affected Domain Concepts:** DC-027 (Background Job), associated operational entities

**Requirements:** FR-023, BR-017, Section 50

**Retryable:** Yes (bounded, per Section 50)

**Critical:**
Failed background task must NOT leave business state inconsistent.
Example: Publishing task fails → content NOT marked as Published.

---

### Concurrency / State Conflict Failures

**Definition:** Concurrent operations conflict on same resource

**Examples:**
- Two users edit same content simultaneously
- Concurrent approval and edit
- Concurrent publishing attempts
- Scheduler duplicate execution

**Business Impact:**
- One operation may fail due to conflict
- State consistency must be preserved
- No data corruption

**System Response (Section 48):**
- Use appropriate locking, uniqueness, transactional, or task-queue mechanisms (Section 48)
- Prevent invalid duplicate processing
- Fail one operation safely
- Preserve consistent state

**Affected Domain Concepts:** Multiple (Content, Schedule, Publishing Attempt, etc.)

**Requirements:** Section 48

**Retryable:** After conflict resolved, may retry

---

### Internal Processing Failures

**Definition:** Internal system errors, unexpected conditions

**Examples:**
- Database connection failure
- Unhandled exceptions
- Resource exhaustion
- Configuration errors

**Business Impact:**
- Operation fails
- User sees error
- System may be temporarily unavailable

**System Response (Section 46):**
- **System shall fail safely** (Section 46)
- **Errors never silently create successful workflow states** (Section 46)
- **User-facing errors understandable** (Section 46)
- **Do NOT expose internal secrets or unnecessary stack traces** (Section 46)

**Requirements:** FR-022, Section 46

**Retryable:** Depends on failure type

## 2.7.4 Failure Severity / Impact Classification

Rather than arbitrary numeric severity scores, failures are classified by **business impact**:

### Blocks Current Operation

**Meaning:** Failure prevents current operation from completing

**Examples:**
- Validation failure blocks approval
- Authorization failure blocks resource access
- AI service unavailable blocks generation

**User Action:** Address failure cause (fix validation, obtain authorization, wait for service)

---

### Blocks Downstream Lifecycle

**Meaning:** Failure prevents content from advancing through lifecycle

**Examples:**
- Research failure blocks generation (when research mandatory)
- AI Review failure blocks progression to next stage
- Validation failure blocks approval
- Missing approval blocks scheduling
- Failed eligibility check blocks publishing

**User Action:** Resolve upstream failure to unblock downstream

**Critical:** Mandatory gates (BR-002, BR-004, BR-005, BR-006) intentionally block progression until resolved.

---

### Affects Single Platform

**Meaning:** Failure affects one platform, others unaffected

**Examples:**
- Platform A publishing fails
- Platform B media validation fails
- Platform C authorization expires

**User Action:** Fix affected platform issue; other platforms proceed independently

**Requirements:** BR-010, BR-011 (platform independence)

---

### Affects Entire Content Item

**Meaning:** Failure affects all platforms or entire content workflow

**Examples:**
- Generation failure (no content to publish)
- Validation failure (all platforms blocked)
- Revision exhaustion (automated workflow stopped)

**User Action:** Address content-level issue

---

### Affects Recurring Campaign

**Meaning:** Failure affects campaign execution or configuration

**Examples:**
- Campaign disabled (BR-015)
- Individual execution failure
- Campaign configuration invalid

**User Action:** Fix campaign issue or disable campaign

**Critical:** Individual execution failure does NOT automatically terminate campaign (Section 31).

---

### Affects User Access

**Meaning:** Failure prevents user from accessing system or resources

**Examples:**
- Authentication failure
- Authorization failure (BR-001)
- Session expiration

**User Action:** Re-authenticate, obtain proper authorization

---

### Requires Human Intervention

**Meaning:** Automated recovery not possible; human decision required

**Examples:**
- Revision exhaustion (Section 21)
- Unknown publishing state (BR-018)
- Permanent publishing failure
- Approval required

**User Action:** Manual action needed (edit content, investigate, reauthorize, approve, etc.)

---

### Recoverable Automatically

**Meaning:** System can retry or recover without human intervention

**Examples:**
- Transient network failure (retry)
- Temporary service unavailability (retry)
- Rate limit (retry with backoff)

**System Action:** Bounded retry (BR-012)

---

### Requires Correction

**Meaning:** Content needs AI correction or manual edit

**Examples:**
- AI Review identifies issues (correction triggered)
- Validation failure (user edits)

**User/System Action:** Correction workflow or manual edit

---

### Requires Reauthorization

**Meaning:** Authorization credentials invalid, user must reauthorize

**Examples:**
- Platform OAuth expired
- Platform credentials revoked

**User Action:** Reconnect platform account

---

**Downstream Impact:**
- Cannot proceed to any lifecycle stage if content not created

**Recovery:**
- User provides valid input
- User obtains proper authorization

---

#### Researching Stage

**Possible Failures:**
- F-003: Research source unavailable, timeout, error

**Resulting State (Section 14):**
- Research marked as **failed** (not successful)
- Research failure recorded with diagnostics
- Actionable failure state exposed

**Downstream Impact:**
- If research is **mandatory**: generation BLOCKED (Section 14)
- If research is **optional**: generation may proceed

**Blocked Downstream:**
- AI Generation (if research mandatory)

**Recovery:**
- Transient failure: bounded retry (Section 14)
- Permanent failure: proceed without research (if optional) or abandon

**Information Preserved:**
- Research failure diagnostics
- Source information (if available)
- Failure timestamp

**Critical:**
- System shall never fabricate research results (Section 14)
- Failed research NOT marked as successful (BR-013)

---

#### Generated Stage (After Generation)

**Possible Failures:**
- F-004: AI Generation service fails, times out, returns invalid/malformed response

**Resulting State (Section 16):**
- Generation marked as **failed** (not successful)
- No new content version created
- Content remains in pre-generation state
- Failure recorded with diagnostics
- Actionable failure state exposed

**Downstream Impact:**
- AI Review #1 NOT initiated (Section 16)
- Content cannot advance

**Blocked Downstream:**
- AI Review #1
- All subsequent stages

**Recovery:**
- Bounded retry only when safe (Section 16)
- Manual content creation

**Information Preserved:**
- Generation failure reason
- Generation input (for retry)
- Failure timestamp

**Critical:**
- Timeouts, invalid responses, malformed responses, provider errors SHALL NEVER be treated as successful generation (Section 16)
- Generation failure does NOT create a "failed" version; content remains unchanged

---

#### AI Review #1 Stage

**Possible Failures:**

**F-005: Technical Review Failure**
- Review service unavailable, timeout, error

**Resulting State:**
- Review marked as failed (operation did not complete)
- Content version NOT reviewed
- Content cannot advance

**Downstream Impact:**
- Correction blocked
- Review #2 blocked
- All subsequent stages blocked

**Recovery:**
- Retry (likely bounded, though not explicitly specified)

---

**F-006: Review Rejection (Business Outcome)**
- Review successfully completes but identifies correctable issues

**Resulting State:**
- Review completed successfully as operation
- Review outcome: issues found, correction required
- Correction workflow triggered (if under revision limit)
- Revision counter incremented

**Downstream Impact:**
- Content proceeds to Correction stage
- Bounded revision rules apply (BR-003)

**Recovery:**
- Automated correction (if under limit)
- If correction succeeds, proceeds to Review #2

**Information Preserved:**
- Review findings (specific issues identified)
- Review timestamp
- Revision attempt number

**Critical:**
- Review rejection is NOT a failure—it's a valid business outcome
- Review succeeded; content needs improvement

---

#### Correction Stage

**Possible Failures:**
- F-007: Correction service fails, times out, returns unusable result

**Resulting State:**
- Correction marked as failed
- No new corrected version created
- Content remains in previous version state

**Downstream Impact:**
- Review #2 blocked (cannot review non-existent corrected version)
- Workflow stalled

**Recovery:**
- Retry (possibly bounded, requirements unclear)
- Manual edit

**Information Preserved:**
- Correction failure reason
- Previous version preserved (Section 19)
- Review findings preserved

**Unresolved:**
- Does correction failure consume a revision attempt?

---

#### AI Review #2 Stage

**Possible Failures:**

**F-009: Technical Review Failure**
- Review service unavailable, timeout, error

**Resulting State:**
- Review marked as failed
- Content version NOT reviewed
- Content cannot advance

**Downstream Impact:**
- Validation blocked
- All subsequent stages blocked

**Recovery:**
- Retry (likely bounded, though not explicitly specified)

---

**F-010: Review Rejection**
- Review completes but identifies issues requiring additional correction

**Resulting State:**
- Review completed, issues found
- **Check revision limit (BR-003):**
  - **If under limit:** Correction triggered, counter incremented
  - **If limit exhausted:** F-008 Revision Exhaustion

**Downstream Impact:**
- If under limit: return to Correction
- If exhausted: workflow enters Intervention Required state

---

**F-008: Revision Exhaustion**
- Revision counter reaches maximum limit (exact value unspecified, Section 74)

**Resulting State (Section 21, BR-003):**
- **Automated revision STOPS**
- **Automatic publishing BLOCKED**
- Workflow enters **Intervention Required** state
- Reason recorded (revision limit exhausted)

**Downstream Impact:**
- All automated progression BLOCKED
- Publishing permanently blocked until manual intervention
- Cannot proceed without human action

**Recovery:**
- **Requires human intervention** (Section 21)
- Manual content edit
- Different generation approach
- Content abandonment

**Information Preserved:**
- Revision count
- Final review findings
- Exhaustion reason and timestamp

**Critical:**
- This is a **safety mechanism** preventing unbounded autonomous operation (BR-003)
- Not a technical failure; bounded automation working as designed

---

#### Final Validation Stage

**Possible Failures:**
- F-011: Content fails validation checks

**Validation Failure Types (Section 24):**
- Missing required fields
- Platform incompatibility
- Media validation failure
- Length/format rule violations
- Missing metadata
- Approval conditions not met
- Other business rule violations

**Resulting State:**
- Validation marked as failed
- Validation findings recorded
- Content NOT eligible for approval
- Publishing BLOCKED (BR-004, BR-005)

**Downstream Impact:**
- Approval blocked
- Scheduling blocked
- Publishing blocked

**Recovery:**
- User addresses validation failures
- Content revalidated

**Information Preserved:**
- Specific validation findings
- Which checks failed
- Validation timestamp

---

#### Approval Stage

**Possible Failures:**

**F-012: Unauthorized Approval**
- User lacks approval authority

**Resulting State:**
- Approval denied (authorization failure)
- Content not approved
- Cannot proceed to scheduling

**Recovery:**
- Authorized user approves

---

**F-013: Approval Invalidation**
- Material edit creates new version after approval granted (BR-007)

**Resulting State (BR-007, Section 12, Section 23):**
- Previous approval **invalidated**
- New version NOT automatically approved
- Content returns to appropriate quality gate
- Invalidation reason recorded

**Downstream Impact:**
- Scheduling blocked (missing valid approval)
- Publishing blocked
- Content must be revalidated and re-approved

**Recovery:**
- Revalidate new version
- Obtain new approval for new version

**Information Preserved:**
- Previous approval record
- Invalidation reason and timestamp
- Version that was approved vs. current version

**Critical:**
- Approval for version N does NOT apply to version N+1 (BR-007)
- Prevents publishing unapproved changes

---

#### Scheduling Stage

**Possible Failures:**

**F-014: Scheduling Failure**
- Content not eligible when scheduling attempted
- Missing approval (BR-006)
- Failed validation
- Invalid timezone (Section 28)
- Unauthorized scheduling

**Resulting State:**
- Schedule not created
- Content not scheduled

**Recovery:**
- Address eligibility issues
- Obtain approval if missing
- Provide valid timezone
- Retry scheduling

---

**F-015: Schedule Execution Failure**
- Content eligible when scheduled, but becomes ineligible before execution (BR-008)

**Execution-Time Revalidation (BR-008, Section 29):**

At scheduled time, system re-checks ALL eligibility conditions:
- Content valid
- Reviews completed
- Validation passed
- **Approval still valid** (may have been revoked)
- **Platform authorized** (credentials may have expired)
- **Media available** (may have been deleted)
- Schedule valid

**Resulting State:**
- If ineligible: **publication REFUSED** (Section 27)
- Schedule remains pending
- Execution failure recorded

**Downstream Impact:**
- Publishing blocked until eligibility restored
- User notified (likely)

**Recovery:**
- Restore eligibility (reapprove, reauthorize platform, restore media, etc.)
- Scheduler retries later (behavior not fully specified)

**Information Preserved:**
- Eligibility failure reason
- Which condition(s) failed
- Execution attempt timestamp

**Critical:**
- Execution-time revalidation (BR-008) is **mandatory**
- Previously valid content may become invalid
- Cannot assume eligibility persists from scheduling to execution

---

#### Publishing Stage

**Possible Failures:**

**F-018: Platform Authorization Failure**
- Platform credentials expired, revoked, or invalid

**Resulting State:**
- Publishing blocked for affected platform
- Connection marked as expired/revoked (Section 33)
- **Other platform connections unaffected** (Section 33)

**Recovery:**
- User reauthorizes platform

---

**F-019: Publishing Failure (Known)**
- Platform API unavailable, rejects content, network error, rate limit

**Resulting State (Section 36, BR-013):**
- Platform Publishing Result marked as **Failed**
- Failure category recorded (transient vs. permanent)
- Safe platform response preserved
- **Failed attempt NEVER recorded as successful** (BR-013)
- **Independent per platform** (BR-010, BR-011)

**Downstream Impact:**
- Publishing to affected platform blocked
- Other platforms unaffected (BR-011)
- Overall status reflects failure

**Recovery:**
- Bounded retry (BR-012) for transient failures
- Permanent failures (content rejected, invalid credentials) not retried as ordinary failures (Section 37)

**Information Preserved:**
- Failure category
- Platform response (safe information)
- Retry count
- Failure timestamp

---

**F-020: Unknown Publishing State**
- Timeout after request sent, connection lost, ambiguous response

**Resulting State (BR-018, Section 47):**
- Platform Publishing Result marked as **Unknown** (not success, not failure)
- **Unknown state SHALL NOT be silently converted** (Section 47)

**Downstream Impact:**
- Publishing outcome uncertain
- Overall status may be Unknown or Pending
- May require investigation

**Recovery (Unresolved):**
- Manual investigation
- Query platform API (if available)
- Time-based assumption (not specified)
- Requires design decision

**Information Preserved:**
- Timeout information
- Request details
- Timestamp

**Critical:**
- **Unknown ≠ Success**
- **Unknown ≠ Failure**
- Explicit unknown state prevents false reporting

---

**F-021: Multi-Platform Partial Failure**
- Content targets multiple platforms; some succeed, some fail

**Example (Section 34):**
- Platform A: Published ✓
- Platform B: Failed ✗
- Platform C: Published ✓
- Overall: Partially-Successful

**Resulting State (BR-010, BR-011):**
- **Platform A result: Published** (confirmed success)
- **Platform B result: Failed** (confirmed failure)
- **Platform C result: Published** (confirmed success)
- Overall status: **Partially-Successful**
- **Each result independent and immutable** (BR-011)

**Downstream Impact:**
- Platform A: success confirmed, no further action
- Platform B: may retry (bounded)
- Platform C: success confirmed, no further action
- User sees accurate per-platform outcomes

**Recovery:**
- Retry only Platform B (failed platform)
- Bounded retry (BR-012)

**Information Preserved:**
- Independent result per platform
- Platform A success preserved despite Platform B failure
- Platform-specific timestamps and outcomes

**Critical:**
- **Platform B failure does NOT overwrite Platform A success** (BR-011)
- Result independence (BR-010) enables accurate partial-success representation

### 2.7.16 Multi-Platform Partial Failure

**Comprehensively covered in F-021 analysis above and Section 2.7.6 Publishing Stage**

**Example Scenario (Section 34):**

- Request sent at T1
- Platform confirms success at T2
- Result recorded: Published
- External ID: platform_a_post_12345
- Status: Success

**Platform B → Failed**
- Request sent at T1
- Platform returns error: Rate limit exceeded
- Result recorded: Failed
- Failure category: Transient
- Retry eligible: Yes

**Platform C → Published**
- Request sent at T1
- Platform confirms success at T3
- Result recorded: Published
- External ID: platform_c_status_67890
- Status: Success

**Overall Status: Partially-Successful**

**System Behavior (BR-010, BR-011):**
1. Three independent Platform Publishing Results created
2. Platform A result: immutable, preserved
3. Platform B result: independent, does NOT overwrite Platform A
4. Platform C result: independent, does NOT overwrite Platform A or B
5. Overall status derived: Partially-Successful
6. Retry targets only Platform B
7. If Platform B retry succeeds → Overall becomes All-Successful
8. If Platform B retry exhausts → Overall remains Partially-Successful

**Critical:**
- **Platform A success MUST BE PRESERVED** even though Platform B failed
- **BR-011 enforcement prevents Platform B failure from overwriting Platform A success**
- User sees: "Published to Platform A and C; failed on Platform B"
- Analytics accurately reflects partial success

**Unacceptable Behavior (Violates BR-011):**
❌ Platform B failure overwrites entire publishing status → "Publishing Failed"
❌ User loses knowledge that Platform A and C succeeded
❌ Analytics incorrectly shows complete failure

### 2.7.17 Unknown External State

**Comprehensively covered in F-020 analysis above and Section 2.7.6 Publishing Stage**

**Example Scenario:**

**Publishing Attempt to Platform X:**
1. System sends publishing request at T1
2. Platform X receives request (system doesn't know this)
3. Network timeout occurs at T2 (no response received)
4. System cannot determine if content was published

**Three Possible External Truths:**
1. Platform X successfully published (system doesn't know)
2. Platform X rejected (system doesn't know)
3. Platform X never received request (system doesn't know)

**System Response (BR-018, Section 47):**
- Record Platform Publishing Result as **Unknown**
- Do NOT assume success
- Do NOT assume failure
- Preserve request details for investigation
- May require manual reconciliation

**Why Unknown State Matters:**

**If treated as SUCCESS:**
❌ Content may not actually be published
❌ False positive in analytics
❌ User believes content published when it wasn't

**If treated as FAILURE:**
❌ Content may actually be published
❌ Retry may cause duplicate publication
❌ False negative in analytics

**If treated as UNKNOWN:**
✓ Accurate representation of system knowledge
✓ Prevents false positive/negative
✓ Enables investigation
✓ Prevents blind retry that could duplicate

**Recovery Options (Unresolved in Requirements):**
- Query platform API (if query endpoint available)
- Manual user investigation (check platform directly)
- Time-based assumption (assume failure after X hours - not specified)
- Wait for user confirmation

**Unresolved Decisions:**
- Exact resolution process for unknown states
- Whether system attempts automatic reconciliation
- How long unknown states persist

### 2.7.18 Retry Analysis

**General Retry Principles:**

**When Retry Appropriate:**
- Transient failures (network issues, temporary unavailability, rate limits)
- Operations that failed due to temporary conditions

**When Retry NOT Appropriate:**
- Permanent failures (content rejected, account suspended, invalid credentials)
- Validation failures (user must fix input)
- Authorization failures (user must obtain permission)
- Revision exhaustion (automated limit reached)
- Unknown state (may cause duplicate)

**Retry Requirements from REQUIREMENTS.MD:**

**BR-012: Bounded and Duplicate-Safe Retries**
- Publishing retries MUST be bounded (Section 37)
- System shall NOT retry indefinitely (Section 37)
- Retries MUST be duplicate-safe (BR-012)

**Retry Mechanisms:**
- Stable job identity (Section 35)

### 2.7.20 Transactional / State Consistency Failures

**Conceptual Scenarios:**

**Scenario 1: External Action Succeeds, Internal Confirmation Fails**

Example:
1. Publishing request sent to Platform X
2. Platform X successfully publishes content
3. System database fails before recording success
4. System believes publishing failed

**Business Impact:**
- Content actually published, but system shows failed
- User may retry, causing duplicate
- Analytics incorrect

**Required Response (Section 47):**
- Unknown external state represented explicitly (BR-018)
- Investigation required before retry

---

**Scenario 2: Internal State Changes, External Action Fails**

Example:
1. System marks content as "Publishing" internally
2. Platform API call fails
3. System must update internal state to reflect failure

**Business Impact:**
- State inconsistency if not handled
- Content stuck in "Publishing" state forever

**Required Response:**
- Update internal state to match reality
- Mark as Failed, not Published

---

**Scenario 3: Partial Multi-Platform Success**

Example:
1. Platform A publishes successfully → result recorded
2. Platform B call in progress
3. System crashes before Platform B result recorded
4. On recovery, Platform A result must remain

**Business Impact:**
- Platform A success could be lost
- Would violate BR-011

**Required Response:**
- Independent result storage (BR-010, BR-011)
- Platform A result immutable and preserved
- Platform B attempt can be retried

---

**Scenario 4: Concurrent Approval and Edit**

Example:
1. User A starts approving content version N
2. User B edits content, creating version N+1
3. Both operations complete simultaneously

**Business Impact:**
- Approval for version N granted, but version N+1 is current
- Must NOT publish version N+1 using version N approval

**Required Response:**
- Version-aware approval (BR-007)
- Approval tied to specific version
- Publishing checks version match

---

**Scenario 5: Material Edit During Scheduled Publication**

Example:
1. Content version N approved and scheduled
2. Before scheduled time, user edits creating version N+1
3. Scheduled time arrives, system attempts publication

**Business Impact:**
- Version N+1 is current, but only version N is approved
- Must NOT publish unapproved version N+1

**Required Response:**
- Execution-time revalidation (BR-008)
- Check current version matches approved version
- Refuse publication if mismatch

### 2.7.26 Failure State Transitions

**Content Lifecycle States with Failure:**

**Normal Flow:**
Draft → Generated → Reviewed → Validated → Approved → Scheduled → Published

**With Failures:**
```
Draft
  → [Research Failure] → Draft (research failed, retry or proceed)
  → [Generation Failure] → Draft (generation failed, retry or manual)
Generated
  → [Review #1 Failure (Technical)] → Generated (retry review)
  → [Review #1 Rejection] → Correction (automated workflow)
Correction
  → [Correction Failure] → Correction (retry)
  → [Revision Exhaustion] → Intervention Required (terminal, requires manual action)
Validated
  → [Validation Failure] → Previous State (must fix issues)
Approved
  → [Material Edit] → Validated (approval invalidated per BR-007)
Scheduled
  → [Execution Eligibility Failure] → Scheduled (pending, wait for eligibility)
Publishing
  → [Publishing Failure] → Failed (may retry if transient)
  → [Unknown State] → Unknown (requires investigation)
  → [Partial Failure] → Partially Published (per-platform results)
```

**Terminal States:**
- Published (success)
- Partially Published (partial success)
- Failed (after retry exhaustion)
- Unknown (requires investigation)
- Intervention Required (revision exhausted)

### 2.7.28 Failure Cascading Analysis

**Cascade Examples:**

**1. Research Failure → Generation Blocked**
- Research fails (F-003)
- If research mandatory: generation CANNOT proceed (Section 14)
- Downstream: Reviews, validation, approval, publishing ALL blocked
- **Isolation:** Failure contained to one content item

---

**2. Review Rejection → Correction → Potential Exhaustion**
- Review #2 identifies issues (F-010)
- Correction triggered (automated)
- If correction fails repeatedly: revision exhaustion (F-008)
- At exhaustion: intervention required, publishing blocked
- **Isolation:** Affects one content item

---

**3. Approval Invalidation → Scheduled Publication Blocked**
- Content approved for version N
- User edits creating version N+1 (F-013)
- Approval invalidated (BR-007)
- Scheduled publication attempts execution
- Execution-time revalidation fails (BR-008)
- Publication refused
- **Isolation:** Affects one content item, one schedule

---

**4. Platform Authorization Failure → Publishing Blocked for That Platform**
- Platform X credentials expire (F-018)
- Publishing to Platform X blocked
- **Isolation:** Other platforms UNAFFECTED (Section 33, BR-011)
- User can still publish to Platform Y and Z

---

**5. Platform B Failure Does NOT Cascade to Platform A**
- Multi-platform publishing (F-021)
- Platform A publishes successfully
- Platform B fails
- **NO CASCADE:** Platform A result preserved (BR-011)
- Platform A success NOT lost due to Platform B failure
- **Critical isolation:** BR-011 prevents failure cascade

---

**6. Unknown Publication State → Investigation Required**
- Publishing to Platform X times out (F-020)
- State unknown (BR-018)
- **Isolation:** Other platforms continue independently
- Unknown state does NOT cascade
- Investigation required only for Platform X

## 2.7.33 Summary and Conclusions

### Analysis Summary

This Error & Failure Analysis has systematically examined **27 distinct failure scenarios** affecting the AI Content Automater system:

**Failure Categories:**
- Authentication/Authorization: 2 scenarios (F-001, F-002)
- Content Lifecycle: 11 scenarios (F-003 through F-013)
- Scheduling: 2 scenarios (F-014, F-015)
- Campaigns: 2 scenarios (F-016, F-017)
- Publishing: 6 scenarios (F-018 through F-023)
- Secondary Operations: 4 scenarios (F-024 through F-027)

### Critical Findings

**1. Unknown State Must Be Explicit (BR-018)**

When external publishing outcome cannot be determined, system must represent state as **Unknown** (not success, not failure). This prevents:
- False positive reporting (claiming success when unknown)
- False negative reporting (claiming failure when unknown)
- Blind retry causing duplicates

**2. Multi-Platform Result Independence is Critical (BR-010, BR-011)**

Platform A success must NEVER be lost when Platform B fails. Independent per-platform results enable:
- Accurate partial-success representation
- Platform-specific retry
- Correct analytics

**3. Bounded Automation Prevents Runaway Operations**

- Revision exhaustion (BR-003, F-008) stops unbounded AI correction loops
- Publishing retry limits (BR-012, F-022) prevent infinite retries
- Campaign execution tracking (Section 31) prevents unlimited content generation

**4. Failure Does Not Bypass Mandatory Gates**

- Failed stages do NOT silently advance content (Section 11)
- Research failure blocks generation when mandatory (Section 14, F-003)
- Validation failure blocks approval (BR-005, F-011)
- Missing approval blocks scheduling (BR-006, F-014)
- Execution-time revalidation prevents publishing ineligible content (BR-008, F-015)

**5. Version-Aware Approval Integrity Preserved**

- Material edit invalidates approval (BR-007, F-013)
- Approval tied to specific version
- System never publishes unapproved version using old approval

**6. Individual Campaign Execution Failure Does Not Terminate Campaign**

- Campaign continues unless user disables (Section 31, F-017)
- Individual execution failures isolated
- Campaign-level vs. execution-level failure distinguished

**7. Secondary Operation Failures Do Not Corrupt Primary State**

- Notification failure (F-024) does NOT change publishing result
- Analytics failure (F-025) does NOT overwrite operational truth
- Primary business operation outcome preserved

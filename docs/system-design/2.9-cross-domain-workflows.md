This cross-domain workflow analysis:

1. **Integrates** domain concepts from 2.5 into end-to-end business workflows
2. **Analyzes** handoffs between domains and required preconditions
3. **Traces** data flow across domain boundaries
4. **Examines** state transitions through complete lifecycles
5. **Enforces** business rules at domain boundaries
6. **Preserves** authorization and ownership through async execution
7. **Distinguishes** successful, partial, and failed outcomes

9. **Documents** unresolved cross-domain decisions

## 2.9.2 Workflow Analysis Principles

### Workflow Definition

A **cross-domain workflow** is a sequence of business activities spanning multiple domain areas that cooperates to achieve a business outcome.

### Workflow Components

Every workflow analysis includes:

1. **Initiating Actor or Event** — Who or what starts the workflow
2. **Participating Domains** — Which business areas are involved
3. **Preconditions** — What must be true before starting
4. **Sequence of Business Activities** — Step-by-step progression
5. **Data Passed Between Domains** — Information consumed and produced
6. **State Changes** — How domain entities transition
7. **Business Rules Enforced** — Which BR-NNN rules apply
8. **Authorization Checks** — Where ownership/permission verified
9. **Successful Outcome** — What success means
10. **Alternative Paths** — Valid variations
11. **Failure Paths** — What can go wrong
12. **Recovery Requirements** — How failures are handled
13. **Audit/History Implications** — What is traceable
14. **Unresolved Decisions** — What remains unspecified

### Failure-Aware Workflow Analysis

Workflows are NOT assumed to always succeed completely.

**Partial Outcomes Must Be Represented Accurately:**

Example: Multi-platform publishing
- Platform A: Confirmed success
- Platform B: Confirmed failure
- Platform C: Unknown external state

This is a **partial success** outcome (BR-010, BR-011), not a binary success/failure.

### Cross-Domain Handoffs

A **handoff** occurs when:
- One domain completes its responsibility
- Another domain assumes responsibility
- Data/state is transferred
- Preconditions for next domain are established

Handoffs are **synchronization points** where:
- Ownership is preserved (BR-001, BR-017)
- Authorization is verified (FR-002)
- Business rules are enforced (BR-001 to BR-018)
- State transitions are validated (Section 11)

## 2.9.5 Detailed Workflow Specifications

### WF-001: Content Creation and Research

**Workflow ID:** WF-001
**Workflow Name:** Content Creation and Research
**Business Objective:** User creates content item with optional research foundation

**Initiating Actor:** User
**Trigger:** User initiates content creation

**Participating Domains:**
1. Identity & Access (authorization)
2. Content Management (content creation)
3. Research (research execution)

**Preconditions:**
- User authenticated (UC-001, FR-001)
- User authorized to create content (FR-003)

**Main Success Flow:**

1. **User Request** → Content Management Domain
   - User provides: title, topic, instructions, target platforms
   - Content Management validates input
   - Creates Content entity (DC-003) with Draft status
   - Assigns ownership to user (BR-001)
   - Returns content identifier

2. **Research Decision** → Research Domain (Optional Path)
   - IF user requests research OR content requires research:
     - Content Management → Research Domain handoff
     - Research Domain receives: content ID, topic, research instructions
     - Research Domain queries configured sources (Section 13)
     - Records source information, retrieval timestamps (DC-005, DC-006)
     - Research results remain distinguishable from generated content (Section 13)
   - ELSE:
     - Skip research; content ready for generation

3. **Research Success** → Content Management Domain
   - Research Domain returns research results
   - Content Management associates research with content
   - Content lifecycle: Draft → Ready for Generation (or equivalent state)

**Data Consumed:**
- User identity (ownership)
- Content creation inputs (title, topic, instructions)
- Research sources configuration

**Data Produced:**
- Content entity (DC-003)
- Research records (DC-005, DC-006) if research executed
- Content ownership association

**Domain Ownership and Handoffs:**

| Handoff | Source Domain | Destination Domain | Information Passed | Precondition | Invariant |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|--------------------|--------------|-----------|
| H-001 | Identity | Content Management | User ID (owner) | User authenticated | Ownership preserved |
| H-002 | Content Management | Research | Content ID, topic, instructions | Content exists | Research associated with content |
| H-003 | Research | Content Management | Research results | Research completed/failed | Results not fabricated (BR-013) |

**State Transitions:**
- Content: Draft → Researching (optional) → Ready for Generation / Research Failed

**Business Rules Enforced:**
- **BR-001:** Content ownership assigned to creating user
- **BR-013:** Research failure not marked successful (Section 14)

**Authorization/Security Checks:**
- User authenticated (FR-001)
- User authorized to create content (FR-002)

**Alternative Flows:**

**A1: Research Not Requested**
- Skip research steps
- Content proceeds directly to generation-ready state

**A2: Research Optional and User Skips**
- User chooses not to research
- Content marked ready for generation without research

**Failure Scenarios:**

**F1: Research Failure (Section 14)**
- Research sources unavailable, timeout, or error
- Research Domain records failure (F-001 from 2.7)
- Research Domain does NOT fabricate results (Section 14)
- Content Management receives failure indication
- Content state: Research Failed
- Downstream Impact:
  - IF research is mandatory: Generation blocked (BR-013)
  - IF research is optional: User may proceed without research
- Recovery: User may retry research or proceed without (if optional)

**F2: Invalid Input**
- Content Management validates input
- Rejects invalid creation request
- Returns actionable error
- No content entity created

**Recovery Behavior:**
- Research failure: Bounded retry for transient failures (Section 14)
- Persistent failure: Content remains in failed research state
- User intervention: Retry or skip research (if optional)

**Downstream Impact:**
- Successful research: Content ready for AI generation (WF-002)
- Research failure (mandatory): Generation blocked
- Research failure (optional): Generation proceeds without research context

**Audit/History Implications:**
- Content creation event recorded (Section 43)
- Research execution recorded with source metadata (Section 13)
- Research failures recorded with diagnostics (Section 14)

**Related Use Cases:**
- UC-004: Create Content Item
- UC-006: Collect Research for Content

**Related FRs:**
- FR-003: Content Creation
- FR-005: Research Collection

**Related NFRs:**
- NFR-001: Security (ownership protection)

**Unresolved Decisions:**

**DECISION WF-001-1: Research Mandatory vs. Optional**
- **Question:** Is research mandatory for all content, optional for all, or content-type-dependent?
- **Why It Matters:** Determines whether research failure blocks generation
- **Affected Domains:** Content Management, Research, AI Workflow
- **Related Requirements:** Section 13 says "support initiating research" but doesn't mandate it; Section 14 says "prevent dependent generation when research is mandatory" but doesn't define when mandatory
- **Impact if Unresolved:** Research failure handling ambiguous

**DECISION WF-001-2: Research Retry Policy**
- **Question:** Exact retry counts, delays, conditions for research failures?
- **Why It Matters:** Recovery behavior
- **Affected Domains:** Research
- **Related Requirements:** Section 14 mentions "bounded retries" but no specifics
- **Impact if Unresolved:** Retry behavior implementation-dependent

### WF-002: AI Generation and Quality Review

**Workflow ID:** WF-002
**Workflow Name:** AI Generation and Quality Review
**Business Objective:** Generate AI content and enforce mandatory two-stage quality review with bounded revision

**Initiating Actor:** User or Scheduler (for campaigns)
**Trigger:** User initiates generation OR campaign execution triggers generation

**Participating Domains:**
1. Identity & Access (ownership preservation)
2. Content Management (version management)
3. Research (context provision)
4. AI Workflow (generation, review #1, correction, review #2)
5. Validation (final validation gate)

**Preconditions:**
- Content exists and is generation-ready
- User authorized to generate (BR-001)
- AI providers configured
- Research completed or skipped (from WF-001)
- Revision limit not exhausted (BR-003)

**Main Success Flow:**

1. **Generation** → AI Workflow Domain
   - Content Management provides: content ID, topic, instructions, research results (if any), target platforms
   - AI Workflow calls AI provider for generation (Section 15)
   - AI provider returns generated content
   - AI Workflow creates Content Version (DC-004) with generated text
   - Version associated with Content entity
   - Content state: Draft → Generated

2. **AI Review #1** → AI Workflow Domain (MANDATORY per BR-002)
   - AI Workflow operates on Generated version (DC-004)
   - Calls AI provider for Review #1 (Section 18)
   - Review evaluates: instruction compliance, factual concerns, relevance, clarity, structure, platform suitability, safety, formatting, configured quality rules
   - AI Review result (DC-007) recorded with: version reviewed, review time, provider/model, outcome, identified issues
   - Review result: PASS or NEEDS_CORRECTION

3. **Decision Point: Review #1 Outcome**
   - **IF Review #1 PASS:**
     - Skip correction
     - Proceed to AI Review #2 (step 5)
   - **IF Review #1 NEEDS_CORRECTION:**
     - Proceed to Correction (step 4)

4. **Correction** → AI Workflow Domain (Conditional)
   - AI Workflow receives Review #1 findings
   - Calls AI provider for correction (Section 19)
   - Correction operates on reviewed version + review findings
   - Correction produces new Content Version (DC-004)
   - Previous version preserved (Section 19)
   - Increment revision counter (DC-009)
   - Content state: Generated → Correcting → Corrected

5. **AI Review #2** → AI Workflow Domain (MANDATORY per BR-002)
   - AI Workflow operates on latest version (corrected or original if Review #1 passed)
   - Calls AI provider for Review #2 (Section 20)
   - Review #2 evaluates corrected content
   - Review result (DC-007) recorded
   - Review result: PASS or FAIL

6. **Decision Point: Review #2 Outcome**
   - **IF Review #2 PASS:**
     - Content state: Corrected → Reviewed
     - Proceed to Final Validation (WF-004)
   - **IF Review #2 FAIL:**
     - Check revision counter against limit (BR-003)
     - **IF revision limit NOT exhausted:**
       - Return to Correction (step 4)
       - Bounded revision loop continues
     - **IF revision limit exhausted (BR-003):**
       - Stop automated revision (Section 21)
       - Content state: Reviewed → Revision Exhausted / Intervention Required
       - Block automatic publishing (Section 21)
       - Record reason (Section 21)
       - **Downstream blocked:** Cannot proceed to approval or publishing without intervention
       - User notified (UC-025)

7. **Final Validation** → Validation Domain
   - Validation checks: required fields, platform compatibility, media requirements, length/format, metadata, approval conditions, business rules (Section 24)
   - Validation result recorded
   - **IF validation passes:**
     - Content state: Reviewed → Validated
     - Eligible for approval (WF-004)
   - **IF validation fails:**
     - Content state: Reviewed → Validation Failed
     - Publication blocked (Section 24)
     - User notified

**Data Consumed:**
- Content, instructions, research results
- AI provider responses
- Revision counter (DC-009)
- Revision limit configuration

**Data Produced:**
- Content versions (DC-004): Generated, Corrected (multiple if revisions)
- AI Review #1 result (DC-007)
- AI Review #2 results (DC-007, potentially multiple)
- Revision counter updates (DC-009)
- Validation result

**Domain Ownership and Handoffs:**

| Handoff | Source Domain | Destination Domain | Information Passed | Precondition | Invariant |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|--------------------|--------------|-----------|
| H-004 | Content Management | AI Workflow | Content ID, context, owner | Content generation-ready | Ownership preserved (BR-017) |
| H-005 | AI Workflow | AI Workflow (internal) | Review #1 findings | Generation complete | Review operates on known version |
| H-006 | AI Workflow | AI Workflow (internal) | Corrected version | Correction complete | Previous version preserved |
| H-007 | AI Workflow | AI Workflow (internal) | Review #2 findings | Correction/Review #1 pass | Review operates on latest version |
| H-008 | AI Workflow | Validation | Reviewed version | Review #2 pass | Mandatory reviews completed (BR-002) |
| H-009 | AI Workflow | Content Management | Revision exhausted | Revision limit reached | Automated revision stopped (BR-003) |

**State Transitions:**
- Content: Draft → Generating → Generated → Reviewing #1 → Correcting → Reviewing #2 → Reviewed → Validated
- Alternative: Generated → Reviewing #1 → (pass without correction) → Reviewing #2 → Reviewed → Validated
- Failure: Any stage → Failed state OR Reviewed → Revision Exhausted

**Business Rules Enforced:**
- **BR-002:** Mandatory AI Review #1 and #2, cannot be bypassed (Section 17)
- **BR-003:** Finite AI revision loops; limit enforced (Section 21)
- **BR-013:** AI failures not marked successful (Section 16)
- **BR-017:** Ownership context preserved in async execution

**Authorization/Security Checks:**
- User authorization to generate preserved in async workflow (BR-017)
- AI providers authenticated with secure credentials (not exposed in results per BR-016)

**Alternative Flows:**

**A1: Review #1 Pass, No Correction Needed**
- Skip correction step
- Proceed directly from Review #1 PASS to Review #2

**A2: Multiple Revision Cycles**
- Review #2 FAIL → Check counter → Return to Correction
- Repeat until Review #2 PASS OR revision limit exhausted

**A3: Background Execution (Async)**
- Generation, reviews, correction execute as background tasks (Section 49)
- Ownership context preserved throughout (BR-017, Section 50)
- Task failures recorded without marking operation successful (Section 50)

**Failure Scenarios:**

**F1: AI Generation Failure (Section 16)**
- AI provider timeout, error, malformed response
- AI Workflow records failure (F-002 from 2.7)
- Generation NOT marked successful (BR-013)
- Content state: Generating → Generation Failed
- Downstream Impact: Reviews cannot proceed
- Recovery: Bounded retry (Section 16) OR user intervention

**F2: AI Review #1 Failure (Technical)**
- AI provider unavailable during review
- Review result: Technical Failure (not PASS or NEEDS_CORRECTION)
- Content state: Reviewing #1 → Review #1 Failed
- Downstream Impact: Cannot proceed to correction or Review #2
- Recovery: Retry OR user intervention

**F3: Correction Failure**
- AI provider fails during correction attempt
- Correction recorded as failed (F-004 from 2.7)
- Revision counter incremented (failure counts as revision attempt)
- Content state: Correcting → Correction Failed
- Downstream Impact:
  - IF revision limit NOT exhausted: May retry
  - IF revision limit exhausted: Revision Exhausted state, publication blocked
- Recovery: Retry (if attempts remain) OR user intervention

**F4: AI Review #2 Failure (Technical)**
- AI provider unavailable during Review #2
- Review result: Technical Failure
- Content state: Reviewing #2 → Review #2 Failed
- Downstream Impact: Cannot proceed to validation
- Recovery: Retry OR user intervention

**F5: Revision Limit Exhausted (BR-003, Section 21)**
- Review #2 returns FAIL
- Revision counter at configured maximum
- AI Workflow stops automated revision (BR-003)
- Content state: Reviewed → Revision Exhausted
- Publication blocked (Section 21)
- Reason recorded
- User notified (Section 38)
- Downstream Impact: **Cannot proceed to approval or publishing** without manual intervention
- Recovery: User must manually edit content OR accept failure

**F6: Final Validation Failure (Section 24)**
- Validation checks fail (missing required fields, incompatible format, etc.)
- Validation result recorded
- Content state: Reviewed → Validation Failed
- Publication blocked
- Downstream Impact: Cannot proceed to approval
- Recovery: User corrects validation issues, re-validates

**Recovery Behavior:**
- AI failures: Bounded retry for transient errors (Section 16)
- Revision exhaustion: Automated revision stops; manual intervention required (BR-003)
- Validation failure: User corrects issues, workflow resumes

**Downstream Impact:**
- **Success (Validated):** Eligible for approval (WF-004)
- **Revision Exhausted:** Publication permanently blocked until manual intervention
- **Technical Failures:** Workflow paused; retries or intervention required

**Audit/History Implications:**
- Generation attempts recorded with outcomes (Section 22)
- Review #1 and #2 results retained for traceability (Section 22)
- Correction iterations recorded (Section 19)
- Revision counter history preserved (Section 21)
- Validation outcomes recorded (Section 24)
- Revision exhaustion event audited (Section 44)

- UC-010: Perform AI Review #2

4. Publishing (execution-time revalidation, publishing)

**Preconditions:**
- Content approved and eligible (from WF-004)
- User authorized to schedule (BR-001)
- Valid execution time and timezone

**Main Success Flow:**

**Phase 1: Scheduling (User-Initiated)**

1. **Schedule Request** → Scheduling Domain
   - User selects: approved content, target platforms, execution time, timezone (Section 28)
   - Scheduling Domain validates: content eligible, user authorized (BR-001), time valid, timezone explicit (Section 28)

2. **Eligibility Check at Scheduling Time** → Publishing Domain
   - Publishing Domain checks: content approved, platforms authorized, media available
   - **IF eligible:**
     - Proceed to step 3
   - **IF not eligible:**
     - Schedule creation rejected
     - Returns eligibility failure

3. **Schedule Persistence** → Scheduling Domain
   - Creates Schedule record (DC-011)
   - Records: content ID, approved version, platforms, execution time, timezone, owner
   - Schedule status: Pending
   - Audit schedule creation (Section 44)
   - Notify user (optional)

**Phase 2: Scheduled Execution (Scheduler-Initiated)**

4. **Work Identification** → Scheduler Actor
   - At/after scheduled time, Scheduler identifies due work (Section 29)
   - Scheduler acquires work safely (Section 29)
   - Concurrent workers prevented from claiming same job (Section 29, Section 48)

5. **Execution-Time Revalidation (BR-008)** → Publishing Domain
   - **CRITICAL:** Scheduler re-checks ALL publication eligibility conditions (Section 29, BR-008)
   - **Publishing eligibility gate conditions (Section 26):**
     - Content is valid ✓
     - Required AI reviews completed ✓
     - Required corrections completed ✓
     - Final validation passed ✓
     - **Required approval exists** — CHECK NOW
     - **Target platform is authorized** — CHECK NOW
     - Required content/media is available — CHECK NOW
     - Scheduled execution is valid — CHECK NOW
   - **IF any condition NOW false:**
     - Eligibility lost between scheduling and execution
     - Publication refused (BR-008)
     - Schedule status: Execution Failed - Ineligible
     - Record reason (approval invalidated / platform deauth / media missing / etc.)
     - Notify user
     - Workflow ends
   - **IF all conditions true:**
     - Proceed to publishing (step 6)

6. **Publishing Initiation** → Publishing Domain
   - Scheduler triggers publishing (WF-008 for multi-platform details)
   - Publishing Domain preserves ownership context (BR-017)
   - Publishing executes per WF-008
   - Publishing results recorded
   - Schedule status updated: Pending → Published / Partially Published / Failed

7. **Result Recording** → Scheduling Domain
   - Scheduling Domain records execution outcome
   - Schedule status: final state (Published / Failed)
   - Audit execution result (Section 44)
   - Notify user (Section 38)

**Data Consumed:**
- Content ID, approved version
- Platform connections
- Execution time, timezone
- Schedule owner
- Execution-time eligibility data

**Data Produced:**
- Schedule record (DC-011)
- Execution result
- Publishing attempts (from WF-008)
- Audit events

**Domain Ownership and Handoffs:**

| Handoff | Source Domain | Destination Domain | Information Passed | Precondition | Invariant |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|--------------------|--------------|-----------|
| H-016 | Identity | Scheduling | User ID (schedule owner) | User authenticated, authorized | Ownership preserved |
| H-017 | Scheduling | Publishing | Eligible content at scheduling time | Eligibility verified | Schedule created only if eligible |
| H-018 | Scheduler | Publishing | Schedule ID, execution time | Scheduled time arrived | Ownership context preserved (BR-017) |
| H-019 | Publishing | Publishing (internal) | Execution-time eligibility check (BR-008) | Execution initiated | Eligibility re-verified |
| H-020 | Publishing | Scheduling | Execution result | Publishing complete/failed | Result accurately reflects outcome (BR-013) |

**State Transitions:**
- Schedule: None → Pending (created) → Executing → Published / Failed
- Content: Approved (remains approved unless edited)
- Publishing Attempts: Created during execution (WF-008)

**Business Rules Enforced:**
- **BR-001:** User authorized to schedule own content
- **BR-008:** Execution-time revalidation of ALL eligibility conditions
- **BR-017:** Ownership context preserved in async execution
- **Section 26:** Publishing eligibility gate enforced independently
- **Section 29:** Concurrent workers safe (no duplicate execution)

**Authorization/Security Checks:**
- User authorized to schedule content (owner, BR-001)
- Ownership context preserved at execution time (BR-017)
- Platform authorization checked at execution time (BR-008)

- Audit cancellation

### WF-008: Multi-Platform Publishing

**Workflow ID:** WF-008
**Workflow Name:** Multi-Platform Publishing
**Business Objective:** Publish approved content to multiple social platforms with independent per-platform results

**Initiating Actor:** Scheduler (from WF-005 or WF-006)
**Trigger:** Scheduled execution time OR campaign execution

**Participating Domains:**
1. Publishing (orchestration)
2. Platform Integration (per-platform API calls)
3. Content Management (version retrieval)
4. Notification (result notification)
5. Analytics (result recording)
6. History & Audit (traceability)

**Preconditions:**
- Content approved and eligible (verified by BR-008 revalidation)
- Target platforms connected and authorized
- Required media available

**Main Success Flow:**

1. **Publishing Initiation** → Publishing Domain
   - Publishing Domain receives: content ID, approved version, target platforms, owner context (BR-017)
   - Creates Publishing Attempt record (DC-017) with overall attempt metadata
   - Identifies target platforms (may be multiple)

2. **Per-Platform Publishing (Parallel/Sequential)** → Platform Integration Domain
   - **FOR EACH target platform:**
     - Platform Integration creates platform-specific Publishing Attempt record (DC-017 per platform)
     - Retrieves platform connection (DC-015, DC-016)
     - Verifies platform authorization valid (token not expired/revoked)
     - Translates content to platform-specific format (Section 32)
     - Verifies media available and compatible (Section 39)
     - Calls platform API to publish content
     - **Platform responds:** Success / Failure / Timeout / Unknown

3. **Per-Platform Result Recording (BR-010, BR-011)** → Publishing Domain
   - **CRITICAL:** Each platform result recorded INDEPENDENTLY (BR-010, BR-011, Section 34)
   - Platform Integration creates Platform Result record (DC-018) per platform
   - Records: platform, status (Published / Failed / Unknown), timestamp, platform response metadata, platform-assigned content ID (if successful)
   - **Independent result storage:**
     - Platform A: Published → Record success
     - Platform B: Failed → Record failure
     - Platform C: Timeout/Unknown → Record unknown state (BR-018)
   - **Platform B failure SHALL NOT overwrite Platform A success** (BR-011)

4. **Overall Result Determination** → Publishing Domain
   - Publishing Domain evaluates overall outcome based on per-platform results:
     - **All platforms successful:** Overall = Published (Complete Success)
     - **All platforms failed:** Overall = Failed (Complete Failure)
     - **Mixed results:** Overall = Partially Published (Partial Success per Section 34)
     - **Any platform unknown:** Overall may be Unknown or Partial, preserving per-platform detail
   - Overall Publishing Attempt status updated

5. **Result Notification** → Notification Domain
   - Notify user of publishing outcome (Section 38)
   - Notification reflects actual state (Section 38)
   - **Never send success notification for failed operation** (Section 38)
   - For partial success: Notify with per-platform detail

6. **Analytics Update** → Analytics Domain
   - Record publishing results in analytics (Section 40)
   - Per-platform statistics updated
   - Overall statistics updated

7. **History and Audit** → History & Audit Domain
   - Publishing attempt recorded in history (Section 43)
   - Per-platform results preserved for traceability
   - Audit publishing outcome (Section 44)

**Data Consumed:**
- Content version
- Target platforms
- Platform connections and tokens
- Media files
- Owner context

**Data Produced:**
- Publishing Attempt records (DC-017, overall and per-platform)
- Platform Result records (DC-018, one per platform)
- Notification records
- Analytics updates
- Audit events

**Domain Ownership and Handoffs:**

| Handoff | Source Domain | Destination Domain | Information Passed | Precondition | Invariant |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|--------------------|--------------|-----------|
| H-030 | Publishing | Platform Integration (per platform) | Content, version, platform, owner | Publishing initiated | Ownership preserved (BR-017) |
| H-031 | Platform Integration | Platform (External API) | Platform-formatted content, token | Platform authorized | Token secured |
| H-032 | Platform (External API) | Platform Integration | Publishing response (success/fail/timeout) | API called | Response validated |
| H-033 | Platform Integration | Publishing | Platform result | Response received/timeout | Result accurately reflects outcome (BR-013, BR-018) |
| H-034 | Publishing | Notification | Publishing outcome | Results recorded | Notification reflects actual state (Section 38) |
| H-035 | Publishing | Analytics | Platform results | Results recorded | Analytics sourced from operational truth |

**State Transitions:**
- Publishing Attempt: None → Publishing → Published / Partially Published / Failed / Unknown
- Platform Results: None → Created (per platform with status)
- Content: Approved (no change; publishing doesn't alter content state)

**Business Rules Enforced:**
- **BR-010:** Publishing attempts tracked and distinguished per platform
- **BR-011:** Failed platform publication does NOT overwrite another platform's successful result
- **BR-013:** Failed publishing never recorded as successful

**Alternative Flows:**

**A1: Single Platform Publishing**
- Only one target platform
- Simpler: Overall result = platform result

**A2: Sequential Platform Publishing**
- Platforms published one at a time (implementation choice)
- Each result recorded before next platform

**A3: Parallel Platform Publishing**
- Platforms published concurrently (implementation choice)
- Results recorded as each completes

**Failure Scenarios:**

**F1: Platform A Success, Platform B Failure (BR-011)**
- **Example:**
  - Content targets: Twitter, LinkedIn
  - Twitter API: Success → Record Platform A (Twitter) = Published

### WF-009: Publishing Retry and Recovery

**Workflow ID:** WF-009
**Workflow Name:** Publishing Retry and Recovery
**Business Objective:** Handle publishing failures with bounded retry and duplicate prevention

**Initiating Actor:** Scheduler (for automatic retry) OR User (for manual retry)
**Trigger:** Publishing failure detected OR user requests retry

**Participating Domains:**
1. Publishing (retry orchestration)
2. Platform Integration (retry execution)
3. History & Audit (retry tracking)

**Preconditions:**
- Publishing previously attempted and failed or timed out (from WF-008)
- Retry limit not exhausted (BR-012)

**Main Success Flow:**

1. **Failure Detection** → Publishing Domain
   - Publishing failure detected from WF-008
   - Publishing Domain evaluates failure type:
     - Transient (rate limit, timeout, temporary platform issue) — May retry
     - Permanent (authorization revoked, content violated policy) — Do not retry automatically
     - Unknown (timeout with unknown state per BR-018) — Handle carefully

2. **Retry Eligibility Check** → Publishing Domain
   - Check retry counter against configured limit (BR-012, Section 37)
   - Check failure type (transient vs. permanent)
   - Check duplicate-safety (Section 35)
   - **IF retry NOT eligible:**
     - Mark publishing failed (final)
     - Notify user
     - Workflow ends
   - **IF retry eligible:**
     - Proceed to step 3

3. **Retry Delay** → Publishing Domain (Optional)
   - Apply backoff delay before retry (Section 37)
   - Prevents rapid retry loops
   - (Exact delay policy: DECISION WF-009-1)

4. **Retry Execution** → Platform Integration Domain
   - Publishing Domain initiates retry
   - Platform Integration re-attempts failed platform(s) ONLY (BR-010, BR-011)
   - **CRITICAL:** Previously successful platforms NOT re-attempted (Section 35, BR-011)
   - Duplicate prevention:
     - Platform idempotency key (if supported by platform)
     - Internal deduplication (Section 35)
     - Check platform for existing content before publishing

5. **Retry Result Recording** → Publishing Domain
   - Platform Integration returns retry result
   - Publishing Domain updates Platform Result (DC-018):
     - Success → Status: Published (retry succeeded)
     - Failure → Status: Failed (retry failed)
     - Unknown → Status: Unknown (BR-018)
   - Increment retry counter
   - Record retry attempt in history

6. **Overall Result Update** → Publishing Domain
   - Re-evaluate overall publishing outcome (Section 34)
   - IF all platforms now successful: Overall = Published
   - IF still mixed: Overall = Partially Published
   - IF all failed: Overall = Failed

7. **Decision Point: Continue Retrying?**
   - **IF retry succeeded:** Workflow complete
   - **IF retry failed AND retry limit NOT exhausted:** Return to step 3 (bounded loop)
   - **IF retry failed AND retry limit exhausted:** Retry exhaustion (step 8)

8. **Retry Exhaustion** → Publishing Domain
   - Retry counter at maximum (BR-012, Section 37)
   - Publishing marked Failed (final)
   - Reason recorded (retry limit exhausted)
   - User notified (Section 38)
   - Workflow ends

**Data Consumed:**
- Previous publishing attempt (DC-017)
- Platform results (DC-018)
- Retry counter
- Retry limit configuration
- Failure type

**Data Produced:**
- Updated Platform Results (DC-018)
- Retry attempt history
- Final publishing status
- Audit events

**Domain Ownership and Handoffs:**

| Handoff | Source Domain | Destination Domain | Information Passed | Precondition | Invariant |
|---------|---------------|--------------------|--------------------|--------------|-----------|
| H-036 | Publishing | Platform Integration | Retry request, failed platform | Retry eligible | Only failed platforms retried (BR-011) |
| H-037 | Platform Integration | Publishing | Retry result | Retry attempted | Result accurately reflects retry outcome (BR-013) |
| H-038 | Publishing | Publishing (internal) | Retry counter check | Retry attempted | Retry limit enforced (BR-012) |

**State Transitions:**
- Publishing Attempt: Failed → Retrying → Published / Partially Published / Failed (final)
- Platform Results: Failed → Retrying → Published / Failed (final)
- Retry Counter: Incremented per retry attempt

**Business Rules Enforced:**
- **BR-010:** Independent platform attempts (only failed platforms retried)
- **BR-011:** Successful platform results NOT overwritten by retry
- **BR-012:** Retries bounded and duplicate-safe
- **BR-013:** Failed retries not marked successful
- **BR-018:** Unknown state explicit (not converted)
- **Section 35:** Duplicate publishing prevented
- **Section 37:** Bounded retries (maximum count enforced)

**Authorization/Security Checks:**
- Ownership context preserved during retry (BR-017)
- Platform authorization re-verified at retry time

**Alternative Flows:**

**A1: Manual Retry (User-Initiated)**
- User manually requests retry after automatic retries exhausted or failure
- Retry counter may be reset (design decision: DECISION WF-009-2)
- User has investigated and resolved underlying issue

**A2: Selective Retry (Multi-Platform)**
- User selects specific failed platform(s) to retry
- Other platforms not retried
- Preserves independent results (BR-010, BR-011)

**Failure Scenarios:**

**F1: Retry Exhaustion (BR-012, Section 37)**
- Configured maximum retry attempts reached
- Publishing remains Failed
- User notified
- Recovery: Manual intervention required (fix issue, manually retry with fresh counter)

**F2: Retry with Unknown State (BR-018)**
- Original attempt: Unknown state (timeout)
- Retry risk: May duplicate content if first attempt secretly succeeded
- **Handling:**
  - Check platform for existing content before retry (if possible)
  - Use idempotency mechanisms (Section 35)
  - If duplicate detected: Mark as Published (original attempt succeeded)
  - If not duplicate: Proceed with retry
- **If unable to reconcile:** Unknown state persists (BR-018)

**F3: Authorization Expired During Retry**
- Platform authorization expires between original attempt and retry
- Retry fails with authorization error
- User notified to reauthorize
- Retry may resume after reauthorization

**F4: Transient Failure Becomes Permanent**
- Original failure: Rate limit (transient)
- Retry: Content violates platform policy (permanent)
- Stop retrying
- Mark failed (final)
- User notified of policy violation

**F5: Duplicate Publication Detected**
- Retry detects content already published (original attempt secretly succeeded)
- Mark Platform Result: Published (original succeeded)
- No duplicate created (Section 35)
- Overall result updated to reflect success

**Recovery Behavior:**
- Transient failures: Bounded automatic retry
- Permanent failures: Stop retrying, notify user
- Unknown state: Careful duplicate prevention
- Retry exhaustion: Manual intervention required

**Downstream Impact:**
- **Retry Success:** Publishing complete; user satisfied
- **Retry Exhaustion:** Publishing failed; user must manually resolve
- **Duplicate Prevented:** Original attempt result preserved (BR-011)

**Audit/History Implications:**
- Each retry attempt recorded in history (Section 43)
- Retry counter and limit tracked
- Duplicate detection events recorded
- Retry exhaustion audited

**Related Use Cases:**
- UC-021: Retry Failed Publishing
- UC-022: Handle Bounded Retry Limits

**Related FRs:**
- FR-016: Platform Result Tracking (including retries)
- FR-017: Publishing Retry

**Related NFRs:**
- NFR-009: Retry Reliability
- NFR-008: Data Integrity (duplicate prevention)

**Unresolved Decisions:**

**DECISION WF-009-1: Retry Delay Policy**
- **Question:** Exact retry delays (immediate, fixed delay, exponential backoff)?
- **Why It Matters:** Retry effectiveness and platform rate limits
- **Affected Domains:** Publishing
- **Related Requirements:** Section 37 mentions bounded retries but not delay policy
- **Impact if Unresolved:** Retry timing implementation-dependent

**DECISION WF-009-2: Manual Retry Counter Reset**
- **Question:** When user manually retries, is counter reset or continuous?
- **Why It Matters:** Retry exhaustion scope
- **Affected Domains:** Publishing
- **Related Requirements:** BR-012 requires bounded retries but doesn't distinguish automatic/manual
- **Impact if Unresolved:** Manual retry counter behavior ambiguous

**DECISION WF-009-3: Retry Limit Numeric Value**
- **Question:** Exact maximum retry count?
- **Why It Matters:** When retry stops
- **Affected Domains:** Publishing
- **Related Requirements:** BR-012, Section 37 require bounded retries but don't specify count
- **Impact if Unresolved:** Retry limit implementation-dependent

**DECISION WF-009-4: Unknown State Reconciliation Mechanism**
- **Question:** How to reconcile unknown state before retry?
- **Why It Matters:** Duplicate prevention
- **Affected Domains:** Publishing, Platform Integration
- **Related Requirements:** BR-018, Section 35, Section 47
- **Impact if Unresolved:** Unknown state handling implementation-dependent

- **Blocked Activities:** Scheduled publishing at execution time (eligibility lost, BR-008)

## 2.9.8 Cross-Domain Invariants

These invariants must hold true across domain boundaries at all times.

### Ownership and Authorization Invariants

**INV-001: User Resource Isolation (BR-001)**
- **Invariant:** User U can access resource R owned by user U' ONLY IF U = U' OR U has explicit authorization to access R
- **Domains:** All domains
- **Enforcement:** Authorization checks at every domain boundary
- **Violation Impact:** Unauthorized data access, security breach

**INV-002: Ownership Context Preservation (BR-017)**
- **Invariant:** Background task executing on behalf of user U operates with U's authorization context
- **Domains:** AI Workflow, Publishing, Scheduling
- **Enforcement:** Ownership context passed and preserved in async execution
- **Violation Impact:** Task executes with wrong authorization, potential unauthorized actions

### Content Integrity Invariants

**INV-003: Version Identity (BR-007)**
- **Invariant:** Approval A applies to specific content version V; approval A is NOT automatically transferred to version V'
- **Domains:** Content Management, Approval
- **Enforcement:** Approval records version; material edit creates new version and invalidates approval
- **Violation Impact:** Wrong content version published

**INV-004: Mandatory Review Completion (BR-002)**
- **Invariant:** Content cannot advance to Final Validation without successful completion of AI Review #1 AND AI Review #2
- **Domains:** AI Workflow, Validation
- **Enforcement:** Validation gate checks review completion
- **Violation Impact:** Unreviewed content published

**INV-005: Revision Bounded (BR-003)**
- **Invariant:** Automated correction/review cycles are finite; revision counter ≤ configured maximum
- **Domains:** AI Workflow
- **Enforcement:** Counter incremented per revision; stop when limit reached
- **Violation Impact:** Infinite loops, cost explosion, workflow never progresses

### Publishing Integrity Invariants

**INV-006: Publishing Eligibility Gate (Section 26, BR-004, BR-005, BR-006)**
- **Invariant:** Content published ONLY IF all mandatory conditions true: valid, reviewed, corrected, validated, approved, platform authorized, media available, execution valid
- **Domains:** Publishing, Validation, Approval
- **Enforcement:** Eligibility check at scheduling time AND execution time (BR-008)
- **Violation Impact:** Ineligible content published

**INV-007: Execution-Time Revalidation (BR-008)**
- **Invariant:** Eligibility re-checked at scheduled execution time; eligibility at scheduling time does NOT guarantee eligibility at execution time
- **Domains:** Scheduling, Publishing
- **Enforcement:** Explicit revalidation step at execution (WF-005 step 5)
- **Violation Impact:** Ineligible content published

**INV-008: Independent Platform Results (BR-010, BR-011)**
- **Invariant:** Publishing attempt to platform P1 and publishing attempt to platform P2 are independent; failure of P1 does NOT overwrite success of P2
- **Domains:** Publishing, Platform Integration
- **Enforcement:** Per-platform result storage (DC-018); independent result recording
- **Violation Impact:** Successful publication erased, reporting incorrect

**INV-009: Unknown State Explicit (BR-018)**
- **Invariant:** Unknown external publishing state is represented explicitly as Unknown, NOT silently converted to success or failure without evidence
- **Domains:** Publishing, Platform Integration
- **Enforcement:** Unknown state recorded when timeout/no response (WF-008 F2)
- **Violation Impact:** Inaccurate result reporting, duplicate publishing risk

**INV-010: Bounded Retries (BR-012)**
- **Invariant:** Publishing retries are finite and duplicate-safe; retry counter ≤ configured maximum
- **Domains:** Publishing
- **Enforcement:** Retry counter incremented; stop when limit reached
- **Violation Impact:** Infinite retries, cost explosion, duplicate publication

**INV-011: Failed Operations Not Marked Successful (BR-013)**
- **Invariant:** Operation that failed is recorded as failed, NOT successful
- **Domains:** All domains
- **Enforcement:** Each domain records actual outcomes (WF-001 F1, WF-002 F1, WF-008 F1)
- **Violation Impact:** Inaccurate reporting, workflow advances despite failure

### Campaign Safety Invariants

**INV-012: Campaigns Follow Workflow (BR-014)**
- **Invariant:** Campaign-generated content subject to SAME review, validation, approval, and publishing gates as manual content
- **Domains:** Scheduling, AI Workflow, Validation, Approval, Publishing
- **Enforcement:** Campaign execution creates content entities that flow through full workflow (WF-006 step 4)
- **Violation Impact:** Unreviewed campaign content published

**INV-013: Campaigns Do Not Create Unlimited Work (Section 31)**
- **Invariant:** Campaign disabled → new future work prevented after disable point
- **Domains:** Scheduling
- **Enforcement:** Disable flag prevents new execution creation
- **Violation Impact:** Runaway work generation, cost explosion

### Notification and Analytics Invariants

**INV-014: Notification Failure Isolation (Section 38)**
- **Invariant:** Notification failure does NOT invalidate primary operation; primary operation result preserved regardless of notification outcome
- **Domains:** Notification, All domains
- **Enforcement:** Notification decoupled from primary operation (WF-010)
- **Violation Impact:** Primary operation rolled back incorrectly

**INV-015: Analytics Sourced from Operational Truth (WF-012)**
- **Invariant:** Analytics derived from operational publishing results (DC-017, DC-018); analytics failure does NOT overwrite operational results
- **Domains:** Publishing, Analytics
- **Enforcement:** Publishing results are source of truth; analytics recomputable (WF-012 F1)
- **Violation Impact:** Operational truth corrupted

### Security Invariants

**INV-016: Secrets Not Exposed (BR-016)**
- **Invariant:** OAuth tokens, API keys, passwords NOT displayed in UI, logs, audit, notifications
- **Domains:** Platform Integration, History & Audit, Notification
- **Enforcement:** Secret filtering at all exposure points
- **Violation Impact:** Credential leak, security breach
